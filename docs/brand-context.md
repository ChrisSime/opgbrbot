# OPG Brbot — brand and content context

## Basic information

* Official name: OPG Brbot
* Consumer-facing brand: BRBOT
* Owner: Damir Brbot
* Location: Lipice, municipality of Brinje, Lika, Croatia
* Elevation: approximately 700 metres above sea level
* Business type: small family agricultural holding
* Production type: traditional mixed agriculture, including plant and animal production
* Production: ecological cultivation, confirmed by the owner

Do not change or expand these claims without confirmation from the owner.

## Story

OPG Brbot is a small family farm located in the village of Lipice near Brinje, in Lika. The farm operates at approximately 700 metres above sea level.

The family grows traditional varieties of vegetables from seed passed down through generations. This inherited seed represents an important part of the family’s heritage and agricultural identity.

The farm uses ecological cultivation. Do not describe the farm or all of its products as certified organic unless certification details are provided.

## Production

Known product categories include:

* cabbage
* kohlrabi

* meat and animal products

Actual availability, prices, packaging, quantities, and delivery options must be confirmed before publication.

## Flagship product

The flagship product is Lički podkapelski češnjak.

Key characteristics supplied by the client:

* traditional local garlic;
* preserved through family seed;
* Damir Brbot saved the variety from disappearance;
* intense aroma and flavour;
* connected to the Brinje and Lika region;
* an important part of the farm’s identity.

The garlic is described as carrying the Lika Quality label.

Do not claim that the garlic variety is officially named after the Brbot family unless documentary confirmation is provided.

The owner confirms that the protection procedure is complete and that the variety is protected as an indigenous, conserved traditional variety.

Safe interim wording:

> Sjeme ličkog podkapelskog češnjaka na gospodarstvu se prenosi generacijama, a njegova se izvornost i tradicijska vrijednost nastoje očuvati za budućnost.

## Fruit production

The farm has approximately 80 plum trees of the Lička bistrica variety.

Plums are processed into products including:

* rakija;
* jam.

Confirm the current number of trees before using it as a prominent or permanent website statistic.

## Lika Quality

Lika Quality is associated with locally produced products from the Lika destination area.

The supplied information confirms the label for Lički podkapelski češnjak. Do not place the label on other product pages unless their certification is separately confirmed.

Use official certification graphics only when the user supplies an authorized, sufficiently high-quality file.

Do not redraw, modify, or imitate a certification logo.

## Brand positioning

BRBOT should be presented as:

* a contemporary local food brand;
* rooted in Lika;
* family-owned;
* focused on traditional varieties;
* committed to preserving inherited seed;
* honest and transparent;
* premium in quality, but not luxurious or pretentious.

The central emotional themes are:

* origin;
* family;
* continuity;
* soil;
* climate;
* preservation;
* honest work;
* local quality.

## Tone of voice

The tone should be:

* warm;
* confident;
* simple;
* specific;
* authentic;
* informative.

Avoid exaggerated, sentimental, or generic agricultural marketing.

Preferred example:

> Na 700 metara nadmorske visine, u selu Lipice, uzgajamo povrće iz sjemena koje se u našoj obitelji čuva generacijama.

Avoid:

> Iz netaknute ličke prirode donosimo vam najzdravije i najbolje proizvode vrhunske kvalitete.
